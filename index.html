Aurora Post is a Christmas match-3 game published by DEVELOPER_OR_COMPANY_NAME. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the game and how you can control advertising privacy choices.

01Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked levels, stars, Aurora Crystals, purchased village decorations, achievements, language, sound and haptic preferences, and endless-mode high scores locally on your device. No account is required.

You can erase this local information through the reset option in game settings or by uninstalling the app.

02Advertising

Aurora Post uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide optional rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements. Rewarded ads are shown only after you choose an offered reward. Interstitial ads may appear after a completed level and are limited by an in-game cooldown.

Depending on your location, consent choices and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals to deliver, measure and protect advertising.

03Consent and your choices

Where legally required, the game displays Google’s consent form before requesting advertising. You can reopen available privacy choices at any time from Settings → Privacy options. Refusing personalized advertising does not prevent normal gameplay; non-personalized or limited ads may still appear where permitted.

You may also manage advertising identifiers and tracking permissions in your Android or iOS device settings.

04Information we do not request

Aurora Post does not require your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings or payment-card information. We do not operate a player-account database and do not sell personal information ourselves.

05Third-party services

Google’s processing is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

06Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children. If the store listing is changed to target children, advertising configuration and this policy must be reviewed before release.

07Retention, security and international processing

Local game progress remains on your device until reset or deletion. Advertising providers retain and process their data under their own policies and may process it in countries other than yours. We use platform security controls and keep data collection limited to what the game and configured advertising services require.

08Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game, advertising setup or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
